Validate and regenerate

Corrupt a copy of the cylinder's required geometry or topology. Rust rejects the defect while the valid display stays visible. Regeneration rebuilds from authored dimensions.


      

      
Validation covers strict v2 structure and sampled curve/pcurve/support residuals. It does not certify general shell self-intersections or cavity containment. Regeneration is not arbitrary BRep healing.
